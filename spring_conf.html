Important Announcement

New for this year! All registrations will be audit by default. This means you will not have to register at the Seminary website. Your class selection, registration, and hotel arrangements will all be made in one location. Watch this space. Registration coming soon.

Those who want to take their class for credit will still have the option to do so. You will have to contact the Seminary, sign up through the SIS system, and pay a little extra. Scholars must be prepared to do extra work!
